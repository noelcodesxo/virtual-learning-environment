# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Self-directed adult learners, especially people studying around careers and responsibilities rather than within a formal school schedule. They need a dependable place to learn from their own materials in short, focused sessions.

## Product Purpose

Study Assistant helps people learn faster and more effectively from their personal library. Success means a learner can turn a source document into a clear answer, a focused practice exam, and usable feedback without losing track of the material.

## Positioning

The product combines question answering and source-grounded exam generation around a learner's own EPUB and PDF library, so study support stays connected to the materials they chose.

## Operating Context

Learners add EPUB or PDF documents, ask questions about indexed material, and generate or revisit practice exams. Use often happens in brief independent study sessions before or after work, alongside professional or personal learning goals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Chat with an indexed library.
- Add, inspect, and remove EPUB and PDF study materials.
- Generate, take, grade, and revisit focused exams from source chapters.
- Preserve the existing Chat, Exams, and Resources workflows during the redesign.

## Brand Commitments

The application should feel minimal, intuitive, and complete. Every control should make its purpose and destination clear. The durable visual direction is "The Focused Study Desk."

## Evidence on Hand

The project contains the working Chat, Exams, and Resources routes, with real loading, error, selection, progress, and success states. No external brand assets, testimonials, or performance claims are available for use.

## Product Principles

- Keep the learner oriented and moving toward the next useful action.
- Make source material, practice, and feedback feel like one connected workflow.
- Favor direct language and understandable feedback over feature density.
- Support meaningful study in short, self-directed sessions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic controls, keyboard access, visible focus indicators, readable type, and reduced-motion support already present in the application.
